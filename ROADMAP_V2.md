# 🚀 Street Brawler Run - Roadmap & Planned Features for Next Release (V2)

## 📋 Saved Feature Requests for Version 2.0:

### 1. 🎁 Power-ups & Weapon Pickups
- **Energy Shield (🛡️):** Protects player from 3 consecutive boss hits with glowing forcefield.
- **Triple Fire Blaster (⚡):** Fires 3 plasma fireballs simultaneously in a spread cone for massive boss burst damage.
- **Speed & Super Jump Shoes (👟):** Doubles run speed and jump height to bypass gauntlet obstacles easily.

### 2. 🤖 Street Minions & Drones
- Dynamic patrolling drones and street bots on Section 1 & Section 3.
- Can be shot or stomped on to clear path and drop power-ups.

### 3. 🪙 Coin System & Character Customization Shop
- Floating 3D rotating neon coins across the level (`🪙 Coins Collected`).
- Lobby Skins Selector:
  - Cyberpunk Ninja (Cyan / Magenta)
  - Golden Mecha Brawler (Gold / Electric Yellow)
  - Shadow Assassin (Dark Purple / Crimson)

### 4. 🎵 Dynamic Interactive Synthwave BGM
- High-energy retro cyberpunk background music during run.
- Seamless transition to intense boss battle orchestral electronic theme in Boss Arena ($Z \ge 150$).

### 5. 🏆 Rating System & High Scores
- 3-Star Performance Rating based on completion speed and zero KOs.
- Best Time Leaderboard banner on Lobby screen.

### 6. 🌌 New Themed Worlds
- **World 2: Space Orbital Platform:** Low gravity floats with neon laser hazards.
- **World 3: Lava Core Foundry:** Collapsing platforms, magma geysers and lava dragon boss.
